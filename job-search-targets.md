# Job search targets

Researched 2026-10-09. Built around the actual plan: keep the Matrix weekend shift
(Thu–Sun, ~26 hrs) as the anchor, add a **remote, weekday-flexible** second role,
keep growing the agency.

Two screening rules apply to everything below:

- **Reject weekend/shift-based roles.** Plenty of remote support work is weekend
  coverage. That collides with Matrix.
- **Check state residence.** Many "remote" postings require living in a specific
  state. Read eligibility before investing in an application.

---

## Lane 1 — Remote part-time IT / service desk

Reliable income on experience already in hand. No ramp-up.

### Pay reality (Oct 2026)

| Role type | Rate |
|---|---|
| General remote helpdesk | $19–30/hr |
| Part-time **contract**, ~20 hrs/wk | **$38–40/hr** |
| On-call desktop support | $27.89–33.59/hr |
| Permanent part-time Tier 1 | from ~$18/hr |

**The pattern worth acting on: contract pays roughly double permanent part-time
for the same work.** A 20 hr/week contract at $38–40/hr beats a permanent
part-time seat at $18–20/hr and fits the schedule better. Favor contract and
temp-to-hire over permanent part-time.

### Search terms that actually surface part-time

"IT help desk" under-returns. These work better:

- `service desk analyst` (part-time)
- `Tier 1 help desk`
- `technical service desk analyst`
- `remote desktop support` + `part time`

### Where to look

- **Dice** — IT-specific, heavy with contract roles. Best fit for the contract angle.
- **Indeed / ZipRecruiter** — use for coverage only; filter hard, expect ghost listings.
- **Staffing recruiters** — still the highest-hit-rate channel, because most
  contract service-desk work never gets posted publicly. TEKsystems,
  Insight Global, Robert Half Technology. One conversation each.
- **Healthcare systems directly** — Epic/MyChart experience is the differentiator.
  Apply through hospital career pages, not aggregators.

## Lane 2 — Automation / AI work (the better hourly rate)

### The find: the n8n community forum

`community.n8n.io` has a **Jobs category** where agencies post paid work and
freelancers post `[FOR HIRE]` threads. This is direct-client, low-competition,
and nothing like bidding against hundreds on a marketplace. A forum regular put
it plainly: Upwork and Freelancer are "overly-crowded."

**This is the least crowded channel found in any of this research.** Start here.

### Other channels

- **Make.com partner/expert directory** and **n8n creator/partner listings** —
  clients check these when hiring. Getting listed is passive inbound.
- **Contra** — portfolio-driven rather than bid-driven; better fit than Upwork.
- **Upwork / Fiverr / Freelancer** — crowded. Worth it mainly to *study how
  competitors position and price*, which is useful for the agency either way.
- **Small agencies and GoHighLevel resellers** — they regularly need white-label
  automation and don't want to build it. Direct outreach.
- **r/n8n, r/make, LinkedIn** — demo posts generate referrals.

### Positioning note

Fixed-price **productized offers** convert better than hourly bids. Two or three
concrete packages (e.g. "lead capture → verification → CRM," "automated weekly
reporting") beat a generic "I automate things" profile. The Facebook-group
intake flow and the OpenAI-to-spreadsheet build are already demonstrable
examples — write each up as a short case study.

## Board notes (what changed since July 2026)

- **Dynamite Jobs** — free *and* screens employers (verifies each role is remote,
  open, and paid). Better value than a paid board for this purpose. **Added.**
- **FlexJobs** — still the most-recommended vetted board, but note the paywall:
  no browsing at all before paying (~$10–25/mo). Fine if you want scam-free
  listings, but you buy before you look.
- **We Work Remotely** — still solid. Employers pay ~$299 to post, which filters junk.
- **Remotive** — still a good curated secondary.
- **Himalayas** — time-zone filtering; not especially useful US-side.
- **Ghost jobs** — a vendor tool claims ~1 in 5 listings are fake or never filled.
  That figure comes from a company selling a detector, so treat it as a rough
  signal, not a measurement. The underlying advice stands: check posting dates and
  look for a named contact before writing a tailored application.

**No evidence found** of major late-2026 changes to board policies or AI-screening
regulation. The 2–3 board strategy (one curated + one volume + recruiters) still holds.

## Order of operations

1. **n8n community forum Jobs** — highest upside, lowest competition. Write the two
   case studies first so there's something to point at.
2. **Three staffing recruiters** — one conversation each, ~an hour total. Ask
   specifically for part-time/contract remote service desk, weekday hours.
3. **Dice** — contract service-desk search with the terms above.
4. **Healthcare career pages** — lead with Epic/MyChart.
5. **Dynamite Jobs + We Work Remotely** — standing searches, low effort.

Use `resume.md` (service desk lead) for 2–4, `resume-automation.md` for 1.
