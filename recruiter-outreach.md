# Recruiter outreach

For TEKsystems, Insight Global, and Robert Half Technology. Contract service
desk work is their core business and most of it never gets posted publicly,
which is why this is the highest-hit-rate hour in the whole search.

**Phone beats email.** Staffing recruiters work a call queue. Use the message
below to open on LinkedIn or email, then get on a call — that's where they
actually match you to a req.

---

## What to say on the call

**Lead with these three** — they're what a recruiter can actually sell:

1. **Healthcare IT, specifically Epic and MyChart.** Named systems. Most
   candidates they see don't have this, and health systems are a steady source
   of reqs.
2. **Tier 2 / Analyst II, not Tier 1.** You provision accounts in Active
   Directory, you don't just reset passwords.
3. **Weekday availability right now, no notice period.** You're adding hours,
   not replacing a job — so you're immediately available in a way most
   candidates aren't.

**Be straightforward about the constraints.** Recruiters would much rather hear
them up front than waste a submittal:
- Weekdays only — can't take weekend-coverage roles.
- Remote.
- ~20 hours, part-time or contract.

**Ask for these specifically:**
- Part-time and contract service desk, weekday hours
- Healthcare clients
- Temp-to-hire is fine (worth saying — it opens more reqs)

**Rate.** We found part-time contract service desk listed at $38–40/hr against
$18–20 for permanent part-time, so **don't anchor low.** Say $30+/hr and let
them come back with the req. If they push for a single number, give a range and
tie it to the role's scope.

**Before you hang up, ask:** "What else should I be looking at with this
background?" Recruiters see the whole req board. They'll sometimes name a role
category you haven't considered — that's free market intelligence.

## Logistics

- Attach **`Chinedu_Ezeofor_Resume.docx`** (the service-desk version, not the
  automation one).
- Expect to be asked for right-to-work and a background check — normal for
  healthcare placements.
- You'll likely get a screening call from a junior recruiter first. Treat it as
  the real interview; they decide whether you reach the account manager.
- One recruiter per firm is enough. Three conversations, about an hour total.
- Don't let two firms submit you to the *same* req — it creates an ownership
  conflict and can get you dropped from both. Ask which client before you
  approve any submittal.
